Each team's number is how many points above or below the league average its games produce, its scoring and its defence together, worked out from this season's games in the league, starting from 30% of its final number last season.

Each league game's total minus its closing line: above 0, the game went over what the market expected. The closing line already allows for the opponent, the venue and the news, so this shows how far the market has been off on a team, not how high its games score. The mean is the long-run average; the median is the typical game, and a gap between them means a few unusual games. Spread is the standard deviation: single games miss the line by about that much either way, so a mean of a few points over a handful of games is description, not proof. The model does not use this: teams against their lines was tested as an extra signal on 7 Nov 2026 and added nothing on top of the team numbers and pace, so it is shown here for context only.

What it cannot see

No news: injuries, rest days and roster moves are invisible to the model until they show up in results.
