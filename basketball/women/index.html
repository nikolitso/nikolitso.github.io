About this page

This page projects totals in women's domestic basketball leagues. Each team gets one number from this season's games: how many points above or below its league's average its games produce. With pace added, the gap between the model total and the line gives the chance a game goes over. A pick is the over only, when the projected total is at least 4 points above the line, in the regular season and on half-point lines.

1H live test records the first-half picks and 1H backtest covers past seasons. Live test records the full-game picks, taken at the opening line, Backtest covers 2021–22 to 2025–26, and Projections shows the next 7 days. A game's line is the median of the main market lines.
