Each game's total minus its closing line: above 0, the game went over what the market expected. The closing line already allows for the opponent, the venue and the news, so this shows how far the market has been off on a team, not how high its games score. The mean is the long-run average; the median is the typical game, and a gap between them means a few unusual games. Spread is the standard deviation: single games miss the line by about that much either way, so a mean of a few points over a handful of games is description, not proof. The model already uses this, weighted by how recent and how many games: it is the signal "The two teams against their lines" above. Counting it again, as a mean, median, home and away split or last five games, did not improve the picks in the 2023–24 to 2025–26 backtest.

What it cannot see

No news: injuries, rest days and roster moves are invisible to the model until they show up in results.
