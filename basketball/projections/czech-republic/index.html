Season record

How it is builtOnly this season's played matches are used. 1. The league average points for home and away sides. 2. Each team's attack and defence against that average (1.00 = average, 1.10 = 10% more). 3. For each match: league average × the home team's attack × the away team's defence, and the same the other way round. 4. Add the two numbers and apply the correction.
The correctionEach league has a small correction in points, set on its past seasons so the model total is not systematically high or low. Overtime points count, because the bookmakers' totals include them.
Early in the seasonA match is projected only after both teams have played 6 games. Before that the ratings rest on too few games to say anything. Each league's rule (over, under or both, and the size of the gap) was chosen on 2022-23 to 2024-25 and then checked on 2025-26.
