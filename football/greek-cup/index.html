Research & methodology

How to interpret the data
Competition and tier definitions

Matches are grouped into SL1 v SL1, SL1 v SL2 and SL2 v SL2 according to each club's division in the source data. Cross-tier matches are treated as the same category regardless of which team is at home.

First-half statistics

First-half figures are calculated only for games containing a first-half data object. The full-match and first-half samples are kept distinct so missing half-time records do not distort the comparison.

Odds and match outcomes

Games with available pre-match 1X2 prices are ranked by the shorter home/away price and split into three similarly sized groups. Over 2.5 prices are grouped independently. Fair probabilities are calculated from inverse odds after normalising across the relevant market outcomes. Actual results use full-time scores. The statistics tables show both teams combined per game; favourite-band tables also separate favourite and underdog output.
