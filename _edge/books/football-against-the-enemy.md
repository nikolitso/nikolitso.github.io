<p>Simon Kuper wrote Football Against the Enemy in his twenties, traveling to more than twenty countries to report on how football reveals the politics underneath it — from Ukrainian ultras and Argentine military dictatorship-era football to Barcelona's identity as Catalan resistance in miniature.</p>

<p>The most cited material is Kuper's reporting from the former Yugoslavia and the Ukraine of the early '90s, where he traces how football clubs functioned as ethnic and nationalist proxies well before the wars that followed made those tensions explicit.</p>

<p>It's journalism from 1994, so some of the political landscapes it documents have since moved on — but that dating is also what makes it valuable now, as a snapshot of football's entanglement with a very specific, volatile moment in European history.</p>

<p>Kuper's reporting method is what makes the political material land rather than feel abstract — he embeds with ultras groups and club officials rather than interviewing them from a journalistic distance, which is how he gets material like fans openly describing a match as a proxy for an ethnic or territorial conflict that official political channels wouldn't let them state as directly.</p>

<p><strong>Key ideas:</strong></p>

<ul><li>Kuper traveled to more than twenty countries in his twenties to report on how football reveals the politics underneath it.</li><li>Football clubs weren't just entangled in the politics around them — for some fans and officials, the club was the most honest way to state a political position no official channel would allow.</li><li>Barcelona's identity as Catalan resistance in miniature and Ukrainian ultras both show football functioning as an ethnic and nationalist proxy.</li><li>It's journalism from 1994, so the political landscapes it documents have since moved on — which also makes it valuable now as a snapshot of a specific, volatile moment.</li></ul>

<p><strong>Worth it if:</strong> you want football writing that treats the sport as a lens on politics and identity, not just tactics and results.</p>
