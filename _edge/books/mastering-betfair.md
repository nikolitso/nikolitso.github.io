<p>This is a practical manual for trading on Betfair's exchange rather than simply betting against a bookmaker — the distinction is the book's entire reason for existing.</p>

<p>Nordsted walks through how exchange odds actually move, how to read market depth, and how in-play trading lets a sharp trader profit from price swings regardless of the final result — a fundamentally different skill from picking winners.</p>

<p>As with most how-to trading guides, the platform-specific screenshots and interface details have aged, so treat the concepts — liquidity, the back/lay spread, scalping, hedging a position — as the lasting value rather than the exact walkthroughs.</p>

<p>The scalping and hedging concepts are the parts most worth extracting from an otherwise dated interface walkthrough — Nordsted's core insight is that exchange trading lets you lock in a profit or minimize a loss by trading out of a position before the event resolves, which is a fundamentally different skill from picking a winner and just as applicable to any liquid prediction market, not only Betfair specifically.</p>

<p><strong>Key ideas:</strong></p>

<ul><li>This is a practical manual for trading on Betfair's exchange rather than simply betting against a bookmaker.</li><li>On an exchange, you don't have to be right about the outcome — you just have to be right about which way the price is about to move.</li><li>In-play trading lets a sharp trader profit from price swings regardless of the final result, a fundamentally different skill from picking winners.</li><li>The platform-specific screenshots have aged, so treat liquidity, the back/lay spread, scalping, and hedging as the lasting value.</li></ul>

<p><strong>Worth it if:</strong> you're moving from fixed-odds betting to exchange trading and want the underlying logic explained clearly rather than a broad survey of betting theory.</p>
