Volleyball Research note

Close matches go under more often than the market expects

23 November 2026 · 36,033 matches · 58 leagues

In the closest fifth of volleyball matches, the under on total points won 53.1% of the time, while the closing prices implied 50.0%. The gap is 3.1 points across 7,209 matches, more than five standard errors, and it appears in both the older and the newer half of the data. It is still not enough to bet on its own: at the best closing price on offer, backing those unders returned −2.4%.

The question

A close match is more likely to need a fourth or a fifth set, and every extra set adds around 40 points. So the total-points line should rise as a match gets closer. If the market prices this correctly, the over should win at the rate its prices imply whether a match is even or lopsided. This note tests whether it does.

How it was tested

  • Matches: 36,033 women's and men's league matches from 58 domestic leagues, seasons 2017-18 to 2026-27 (up to 18 November 2026), with closing prices for both the winner and the total points.
  • Closeness: from the closing match-winner prices with the margin removed. 1.00 is an even match, 0 a certain one. The matches are split into five equal groups.
  • The line and its chance: the median main line across the market, and the average of the closing over and under prices at that line, with the margin removed.
  • Matches that finished exactly on the line are left out.

The line does rise with closeness, from 135 points in the most lopsided fifth to 183 in the closest. The share of five-set matches stops rising before the line does: 28.9% in the fourth group and 29.2% in the closest. The under won more often than implied in every group except the most lopsided, and by the most where matches were closest.

Does it hold up?

  • Older seasons (2017-18 to 2021-22): in the closest fifth the under beat its implied rate by 4.0 points.
  • Newer seasons (2022-23 on): 2.7 points, across 4,418 matches, about 3.5 standard errors.
  • Only matches with two or more prices at the line: 2.2 points, about 2.6 standard errors. Smaller, same direction.
  • By league: only two leagues have 300 or more matches in the closest group: Ligue A in France (+3.3 points) and Serie A2 women in Italy (+0.3). There is not enough data per league to say whether the pattern is spread across the market or concentrated in a few leagues.

Can it be bet?

Not on its own. Backing the under in the closest fifth at the best closing price on offer returned −2.4% (95% range ±2.1 points, 7,207 matches). At the opening line and best opening price it returned −2.3%, and in the newer seasons −3.1%. For comparison, the same bet in the most lopsided fifth returned −8.7%. The pattern removes most of the loss, but the margin is still larger than the edge.

What it is for

A check on the volleyball totals model showed it already sits further under than the market in close matches, and too far: it gave the over a 41% chance where the over won 47%. So the market's error is not one the model shares, and shading its totals down would make it worse. The live picks, overs on lines up to 155.5, come almost entirely from lopsided matches, where neither the market nor the model shows this bias. Since 23 Nov 2026 the totals shown on the volleyball page include a correction for this; the picks still use the uncorrected model.

Limits

  • The data has no set-betting prices, so whether the set markets carry the same error is untested.
  • The implied chance uses average closing prices; the betting test above uses the best single price.
  • Results are for league matches only; cups and international games are not included.
  • Data: the site's own collection of results and market prices.
