Volleyball Explainer

Why a volleyball total is really about sets

24 November 2026 · 64,184 matches · 64 leagues

A volleyball total does not cluster around one number. It forms three humps, one for each length of match: 3 sets average 131 points, 4 sets 181 and 5 sets 210. The overall average, 163, falls in the gap between them: only 2.5% of matches end within five points of it. So an over/under line is mostly a question about how many sets will be played.

Nearly half of all matches end in three sets, and those finish between 120 and 142 points eight times in ten. A fourth set adds about 50 points, and a fifth about 29 more, because a deciding set is played to 15 instead of 25. Between the 3-set and 4-set humps there is a gap: only 1.3% of matches end between 150 and 159 points.

Sets do the work, not rallies

Points per set barely change with the length of the match: 43.7 in 3-set matches, 45.2 in 4-set matches and 41.9 in 5-set matches, where the short fifth set pulls the average down. Two teams that trade long sets add a few points to the total. One extra set adds around 50. That is why the main thing to estimate for a volleyball total is the chance of each match length, and that chance depends on how evenly matched the two teams are.

Conegliano were heavy favourites, so the model expected three sets four times in five. Its median, 123 points, sits inside the 3-set hump, and so did the market's line of 130.5. For that line the question was how long the three sets would run: in the data, 56% of 3-set matches go over 130.5. The match ended 3–0 with 138 points, over the line.

Katowice against Częstochowa looked even, so every length had about a one-in-three chance. The line of 182.5 sat in the 4-set hump, and in practice the over needed a fourth set. The match ended 3–0 with 132 points, 50 under the line. The points per set were normal; the fourth set never came.

What this means for a line

  • A line near the average is a line in the gap. A total of about 160 is rarely the score. It works as a question: will this match go beyond three sets?
  • The median is a better guide than the average. In a match with mixed chances the average lands between the humps; the median lands on a total that actually happens.
  • Even matches are the uncertain ones. When each length is about equally likely, the total can end anywhere from 120 to 220, and a small change in the set chances moves the fair line a long way.
