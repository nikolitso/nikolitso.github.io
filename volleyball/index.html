About this page

Projections for women's and men's volleyball league matches: the chance each ends in three, four or five sets, its total points and its points margin, from one rating per team. Four kinds of pick are logged before the first serve and kept on Live test: the over on a low total, the handicap favourite, the handicap underdog and the match winner; How it works has the rules.

Projections shows the next 7 days, Season Ratings every league's teams and Backtest the past seasons since 2020. Every game has its own page.

Explainer: why a volleyball total is really about sets.

Research note: close matches go under more often than the market expects.
