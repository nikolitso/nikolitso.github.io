About this page

This page projects women's and men's volleyball league matches: the chance each one ends in three sets or goes to four or five, and its total points. Every team gets one rating, its chance of winning a rally against an average team in its own league, from this season's matches only. A pick is an over on a low line, where the market expects a 3-0, when the model's edge clears the page's bar.

Projections shows the next 7 days against the market line, and Live test records the picks, logged before the first serve. Season, Backtest and Leagues show the ratings, past seasons since 2020 and each league's market. Every game has its own page.
