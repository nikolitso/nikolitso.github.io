About this page

This page projects women's and men's volleyball league matches: the chance each one ends in three sets or goes to four or five, and its total points. Every team gets one rating, its chance of winning a rally against an average team in its own league, from this season's matches only. There are three kinds of pick: the over on a low total line, where the market expects a 3-0, and the favourite or the underdog on the points handicap, each when the model's edge clears the page's bar and the league's competitiveness allows it.

Projections shows the next 7 days against the market line, and Live test records the picks, logged before the first serve. Season Ratings and Backtest show every league's team ratings and past seasons since 2020. Every game has its own page.

The projected totals, fair lines and 3/4/5-set chances include a correction fitted on past seasons: the model's sets run short in close matches, so up to 3 points are added there, and its 5-set chances are trimmed. Picks use the model without this correction.
