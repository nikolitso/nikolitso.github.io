About

Football Trading Team Leader, pricing pre-event soccer markets at scale.

The work combines pre-event pricing and quantitative modelling with building the teams and processes that turn data into prices.

The same discipline carries into independent projects: analytics hubs for my favourite team, PAOK.

Alongside them, two publications on subjects that reward patience. What Algo Missed is an independent guide to world cinema, curated film by film and never sponsored. The Edge distils books on analytics, decision-making and management into practical takeaways.
