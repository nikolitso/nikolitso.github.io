About

Turning data into prices, and curiosity into ideas worth sharing.

Football Trading Team Leader, based in Thessaloniki, Greece. More than 25 years around football and betting markets: from one of the first betting newspaper pullouts in Greece to leading a team that prices pre-event soccer markets today.

The day job is pre-event pricing and quantitative modelling: building the models, and the teams and processes, that turn data into prices for football markets at scale.

Career

It started with chemical engineering, left unfinished: a career on the factory floor was never the plan, and betting markets were a far better fit.

In 2000 that meant newsprint, managing a team of betting writers for one of the first dedicated betting newspaper pullouts in Greece. From 2008 to 2017 came nine years producing and managing sports betting content at scale for one of Europe's leading operators.

In 2018 the move from writing about prices to setting them: five years as a soccer trader, combining statistical analysis with real-time pricing decisions and probability assessment across soccer markets. Since 2023, leading a team of traders in a soccer pre-event team, with full ownership of pricing quality, model development and team performance.

Outside work

The same discipline carries into everything else. The Edge is a working library of more than 100 books on analytics, betting, decision-making, management and personal development, each distilled into practical takeaways.

What Algo Missed is a personal map of world cinema: nearly 200 films from more than 30 countries, chosen one by one, far from what the algorithm would recommend.

And then there's PAOK, a lifelong passion. PAOK Hub follows the football team season by season: how it plays, how the players perform, and how the results compare with what the numbers expected. PAOK Basketball does the same for the basketball team, with the roster, schedule, team and player stats, and standings all in one place.

Elsewhere: LinkedIn, X and Letterboxd. To get in touch, write to contact@antonisnikolitsopoulos.com.
