About this page

This page compares PAOK with the Big-3: AEK, Olympiakos and Panathinaikos. Each row puts PAOK's per-game figure for the 2026–27 Super League beside the average of those three clubs, in six groups from General to Goalkeeping. Expected goals (xG) opens the Attack table and expected goals against (xGA) opens Defence.

The Verdict column says whether PAOK is Better or Worse, with the gap in brackets; for percentages the gap is in percentage points, and gaps under 0.05 read Even. Some stats count as better when lower, such as cards, fouls and shots faced on target. PAOK vs Rest runs the same rows against the rest of the league.
