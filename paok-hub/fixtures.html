About this page

This page lists PAOK's upcoming matches across all competitions in 2026–27. Each card gives the competition, the two teams with the home side named first, and the kickoff day, date and time in Athens time. Super League games carry their matchday number; Greek Cup ties are labelled as such.

The card at the top is the next match. The hub's front page uses it for its Next match banner, and PAOK vs Next Opponent reads it to decide which team to set against PAOK.

SL = Super League. Kickoff times local (Athens). Cup kickoff times to be confirmed closer to the date.
