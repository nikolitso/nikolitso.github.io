About this page

Opponents Stats looks at each Super League club from the other side of the ball. For all 14 teams in 2026–27 it gives per-game averages for what their opponents did against them: duels and aerials won, passes and crosses, shots faced and goals conceded, tackles, fouls and cards, and the opposing goalkeeper's saves, claims and punches.

There are six tables: General, Distribution, Attack, Defence, Discipline and Goalkeeping. Clicking a column header sorts the table and renumbers the rank. PAOK's row is highlighted. The same columns for each team's own output are on Teams Stats. The figures come from Opta match data.
