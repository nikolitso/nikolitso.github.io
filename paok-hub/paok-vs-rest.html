About this page

PAOK vs Rest measures PAOK against the ten Super League teams outside the Big-3. The comparison figure is the average of every club except PAOK, AEK, Olympiakos and Panathinaikos, taken per game over the 2026–27 season.

The stats are split into General, Attack, Defence, Distribution, Discipline and Goalkeeping, with xG and xGA included. Each row ends in a verdict, an upward arrow for Better or a downward one for Worse, judged by whether a higher or a lower value is preferable for that stat. Read it next to PAOK vs Big-3, which sets the same rows against the average of AEK, Olympiakos and Panathinaikos.
