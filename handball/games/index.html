Each team gets an attack and a defence rating from its league's games so far this season. 1.000 is the league average: an attack of 1.100 scores 10% more than its opponents usually concede, and a defence of 0.900 lets in 10% fewer than its opponents usually score. The ratings allow for who each team has played, and each one is pulled towards 1.000 as if the team had also played a few average games, so a small number of games does not swing it too far. A team's goals are the league average for its side times its attack and the opponent's defence; the two are added, and a league correction (how far that league's real totals ran from the raw figure, 2022-23 to 2024-25) gives the model total. In the Bundesliga and 2. Bundesliga a shooting and pace correction follows (from 19 Nov 2026): goals come back towards normal when opponents have been shooting unusually well or badly against the two teams, and go up when the two teams play more possessions a game than the league average. Each game's page shows these steps with its own numbers; figures are rounded, so the last digit may differ.
