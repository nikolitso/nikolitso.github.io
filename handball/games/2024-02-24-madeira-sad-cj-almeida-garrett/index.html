How the model got its total

Each team gets an attack and a defence rating from this season's games before this one (Madeira SAD 17, CJ Almeida Garrett 17). 1.000 is league average: an attack of 1.100 scores 10% more than its opponents usually concede, and a defence of 0.900 lets in 10% fewer than its opponents usually score. The ratings allow for who each team has played, and each one is pulled towards 1.000 as if the team had also played 0 average games, so a few games do not swing it too far. Figures are rounded, so the last digit may differ.
