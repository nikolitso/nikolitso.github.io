About this page

This page lists every handball league the model follows in the 2026-27 season, with games played and average goals a game. Each league has its own page with three tables.

League averages gives total goals, the home and away sides' goals, home wins, draws and away wins, and the model's league correction. Standings is the regular-season table, with 2 points for a win and 1 for a draw. Team averages shows goals scored and conceded, home and away totals, and the model's attack and defence ratings, where 1.000 is the league average. A team's projected goals are the league average for its side times its attack times the opponent's defence.
