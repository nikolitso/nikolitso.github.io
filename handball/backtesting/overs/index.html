How it works

  1. Each team gets attack and defence ratings from this season's results only.
  2. A match is flagged when the model total is 2+ goals beyond the market's line, once both teams have played 6 games. Each league has one side: Over or Under.
  3. A pick is logged once, with the line and odds at that moment, and settled at that price, at equal stakes. ROI = profit ÷ total staked on won and lost picks; pushes are left out.

Calibration

Is the model's chance right? The same picks, comparing the chance the model gave with how often the pick actually won. The chance assumes the real total lands around the model's total with that league's usual spread, measured on 2022-23 to 2024-25. Actual − model is in percentage points: near zero means the chance can be trusted, negative means the model was too confident. Price implies is 1 ÷ the average price. Small bands move a lot by chance.
