The Big 4 after a European game

Do Olympiakos, PAOK, AEK and Panathinaikos play differently three or four days after a European game? Every league game from 2016–17 to now.

2.56goals a game when a Big-4 side hosts a small club after Europe (2.84 rested) 48%of those home games went over 2.5 (58% rested) 45%over 2.5 after Europe, home and away. The odds expected 52% +1 carda game in derbies after Europe (6.5 against 5.5)

In short

  • Against small clubs the Big-4 side still wins as often, but scores less. Fewer goals, mostly at home.
  • The odds miss it: overs land 6–7 points less often than they expect.
  • In derbies the result doesn't change, but there is about one card more.
  • Three or four days of rest makes no difference.
