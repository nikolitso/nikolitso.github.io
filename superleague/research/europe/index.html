The Big 4 after a European game

What happens when Olympiakos, PAOK, AEK or Panathinaikos play a league game three or four days after a European match? This covers the Super League from 2016–17 to 18 Oct 2026 and the Big 4's 416 European games in that time.

The Big-4 side still wins as often, but scores less, so the game has fewer goals. The drop is clear at home: 2.84 goals a game falls to 2.56, and over 2.5 from 58% to 48%. Away it is small.

The raw averages slightly understate it, because the teams that play in Europe are usually the strongest that season. Comparing each team only with its own other games in the same season, games after Europe have about 0.5 fewer goals at home and 0.2 fewer away. The Big-4 side's own goals drop by 0.5 at home and 0.3 away, while it concedes no more.

A tired Big-4 side still wins about as often as the odds say. But its games went over 2.5 goals far less often than the odds expected. With only 67 games, this is worth watching, not yet proven.

Derbies: no effect on the result

Since 2016–17 there have been 207 derbies between the Big 4. In 27 of them, only one side had played in Europe three or four days earlier. That side did about as well as usual:

That is about one extra card per game, and one to one and a half extra fouls. It was stronger in recent seasons (about 1.3 extra since 2021–22, 0.4 before), and 27 games is still a small number, so treat it as a lead, not a rule.

The other 12 derbies, where both sides had played in Europe that week, are left out of both tables. *Fouls only since 2019–20 (140 and 18 derbies), as earlier foul counts are missing.

What changed in the model

  • Nothing for European games, for now. The odds already price the result, and the goals and derby cards findings rest on small samples. Both will be checked again as games come in.
  • Fouls by type of game, added on 8 Nov 2026. While checking this, Big-4 v small games turned out to have about 1.5 fewer fouls than the model projected, every season. Their projected fouls are now cut by about 4%. See How it works.

About the data

  • Super League games from 2016–17 to 18 Oct 2026 and every European game the Big 4 played in that time, qualifiers included (416). xG starts in 2020–21.
  • "After Europe" means the league game came three or four days after a European game. A small club is any team outside Olympiakos, PAOK, AEK and Panathinaikos.
