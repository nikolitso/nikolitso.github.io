The Big 4 after a European game

What happens when Olympiakos, PAOK, AEK or Panathinaikos play a league game three or four days after a European match? This covers the 2,446 Super League games from 2016–17 to 18 Oct 2026 and the Big 4's 416 European games in that time. The question came up before Olympiakos – PAOK on 8 Nov 2026, three days after Olympiakos played in Europe while PAOK rested.

Against a small club the tired side scores about 0.4 goals fewer but concedes no more. It doesn't defend worse. It creates less, and the game has fewer goals. Points drop less than goals because most of these games are still won.

Three findings:

  • Small-club games after Europe: about 0.3 fewer goals and 0.45 fewer shots on target than projected. That fits the first table.
  • Derbies in general: the model gets them right. There's no bias in any stat over 121 derbies.
  • Fouls: every Big-4 v small game had about 1.5 fewer fouls than projected, with or without Europe. This led to a change in the model (see the last section).

The result is priced. The closing odds already make a tired Big-4 side a slightly smaller favourite, and they win as often as the odds say. The goals aren't priced: the over 2.5 implied chance barely moves, but it landed 13 points less often. 67 games is a small sample (±6 points), so this is a lead to watch, not a proven edge.

Derbies: the result doesn't move, the cards might

In the 15 derbies since 2021–22 with a tired side, there were 1.4 more cards and 2.8 more fouls than the model projected. A plausible reason is tired legs arriving late in a game that is already tense. But 15 games is very few, and over all ten seasons the tired side's own cards rose by only 0.28 (±0.22). Foul counts from 2016–19 also look unreliable. So this is a lead, not a rule.

What changed in the model

  • No European-rest factor, for now. In small-club games the 1X2 is already priced, and the −0.28 goals against the model is only about two standard errors on 116 games. The derby cards effect rests on 15 games. Both will be checked again as games come in.
  • A fouls factor by type of game was added on 8 Nov 2026. It came out of the replay above. Projected fouls are multiplied by about ×0.96 in Big-4 v small games, ×1.01 in derbies and ×1.02 between small clubs, before the referee's factor. Set from earlier seasons only, it cut the miss in four of the five seasons tested. It is recalculated on every update. See How it works.

About the data

  • Games: Super League league games from 2016–17 to 18 Oct 2026, from the site's own match data. xG starts in 2020–21.
  • European games: every Champions League, Europa League, Conference League and qualifier game the Big 4 played in that time (416), from public team schedules. "After Europe" means the league game came three or four days after a European game (never fewer in this data).
  • Small clubs: every team outside Olympiakos, PAOK, AEK and Panathinaikos. A derby is a game between two of the Big 4.
  • ± is one standard error. In the first table it comes from resampling whole games.
