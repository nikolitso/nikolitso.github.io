Greek Super League Explainer

How dominant are the Big 4 in the Greek Super League?

Olympiakos, PAOK, AEK and Panathinaikos win most of their games against the rest of the league. That part is no surprise; the size of the gap is. Against the Big 4, the other clubs scored 23% fewer goals than their own form predicted, and the Big 4 scored 14% more than theirs. The shortfall has shown up in every season since 2019-20.

More than half the time, the smaller club did not score at all. But results alone cannot say whether that is anything more than the Big 4 simply being better teams.

Measured against form

To separate the two, each club gets an attacking and a defensive rating from its earlier league games only, and every match gets an expected score before it is played: the goals each side would score if both played to their form. Over a season, a club's goals should match its expected goals, whoever the opponent. In these games they do not.

Smaller clubs fell short against the Big 4 in all 8 seasons, by between 4% (2021-22) and 39% (2022-23). The Big 4 scored more than their form in 6 of the 7 full seasons, though only just in 2025-26 (+1%), and less in 2024-25 (−9%).

Fewer chances, and worse finishing

Expected goals (xG) rate each shot by how often a chance like it is scored. On the 565 matches with xG data, smaller clubs created 15% less xG against the Big 4 than their form predicted, and scored 20% fewer goals. So about three parts in four of the gap are fewer and poorer chances; the rest is finishing below the chances they did get.

What it means for predictions

A rating built from overall form treats a game against Olympiakos like a game against any strong side, so it overrates the smaller club in these matches. The Super League projections on this site correct for this, but only by half: a smaller club's expected goals against the Big 4 are cut by about 10%, and the Big 4's raised by about 6%. Half was chosen by testing a quarter, a half and the full gap season by season, each time using only earlier seasons; half did best. Part of any one season's gap is chance, so carrying all of it forward over-corrects.

Limits

  • "Smaller club" means every club outside the Big 4, from the top of the rest to the bottom of the table.
  • Each season has 80–96 of these matches, so single seasons swing.
  • Form here is the site's own rating of each club; another rating method would give somewhat different numbers.
  • Data: 1,718 league matches from 2019-20 to round 7 of 2026-27.
