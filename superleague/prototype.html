How to read the signal

A positive difference is a screening flag, not a recommendation.

Probability edge
Compare the model's estimated probability with the market's raw implied probability. A positive difference identifies a price worth further inspection.
Market margin
A production view should also calculate no-vig market probabilities across all outcomes before classifying value.
Qualification rules
Minimum edge, price freshness, market liquidity, sample reliability and model calibration should be explicit before a signal becomes a published pick.
