About this page

This hub follows PAOK basketball in the 2026-27 season, in the Greek Basket League and the BKT EuroCup. Home shows the next game and the last result. Next Game has a scouting report on the opponent and a projected score. Schedule lists results and fixtures. Team Stats and Player Stats give box-score figures, and Player Profiles rates each player's offence and defence against others in the same competition. Coach collects the head coach's words before and after games.

Standings adds a power ranking. Power is points per 100 possessions better than an average team, corrected for opponents and home court. Expected wins are the wins a team deserves from points scored and allowed.
