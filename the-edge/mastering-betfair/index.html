Mastering Betfair

The mechanics of trading odds on an exchange rather than just betting them.

by Pete Nordsted · 2009

Share on X

This is a practical manual for trading on Betfair's exchange rather than simply betting against a bookmaker — the distinction is the book's entire reason for existing.

Nordsted walks through how exchange odds actually move, how to read market depth, and how in-play trading lets a sharp trader profit from price swings regardless of the final result — a fundamentally different skill from picking winners.

As with most how-to trading guides, the platform-specific screenshots and interface details have aged, so treat the concepts — liquidity, the back/lay spread, scalping, hedging a position — as the lasting value rather than the exact walkthroughs.

The scalping and hedging concepts are the parts most worth extracting from an otherwise dated interface walkthrough — Nordsted's core insight is that exchange trading lets you lock in a profit or minimize a loss by trading out of a position before the event resolves, which is a fundamentally different skill from picking a winner and just as applicable to any liquid prediction market, not only Betfair specifically.

Key ideas

  • This is a practical manual for trading on Betfair's exchange rather than simply betting against a bookmaker.
  • On an exchange, you don't have to be right about the outcome — you just have to be right about which way the price is about to move.
  • In-play trading lets a sharp trader profit from price swings regardless of the final result, a fundamentally different skill from picking winners.
  • The platform-specific screenshots have aged, so treat liquidity, the back/lay spread, scalping, and hedging as the lasting value.
Worth it if

you're moving from fixed-odds betting to exchange trading and want the underlying logic explained clearly rather than a broad survey of betting theory.
