How To Watch Football

52 small rules for noticing more of what's actually happening in a match.

by Tifo · 2022

Share on X

Tifo Football built a following on YouTube doing tactical breakdowns that assume you already like football but want to understand what you’re actually looking at — why a back three shifts the way it does, what a false nine is really solving for, why a manager’s substitution pattern isn’t random. How To Watch Football is that same instinct in book form: 52 bite-sized rules for watching more like a coach and less like a fan following the ball.

It’s a lighter, more practical companion to the heavier analytics books on this shelf — less about proving a statistical point, more about training your eye during a match you’re already watching live.

The 52-rule structure means each idea gets isolated and concrete rather than folded into a larger system — a rule about reading a fullback's body shape before a cross stands on its own, testable the next time you watch a match, rather than requiring you to first absorb a whole tactical framework. That's a deliberate tradeoff: less theoretical coherence, more immediately usable in the next 90 minutes you watch.

Key ideas

  • 52 bite-sized rules train you to watch more like a coach and less like a fan following the ball.
  • You get better at watching football the same way you get better at anything — by knowing specifically what to look for.
  • Each rule is isolated and concrete rather than folded into a larger theoretical system, so it's testable the next match you watch.
  • It's a lighter, more practical companion to the heavier analytics books on this shelf.
Worth it if

you want to get more out of the games you’re already watching this weekend, not build a model.
