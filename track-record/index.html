Every live test on the site in one table. Each pick is logged before the start, at the price shown on its project page, and settled on the final score. Two numbers judge a test: ROI, what the picks returned, and closing-line value (CLV), whether they were taken at a better price than the market closed at.

How to read the table

  • ROI is profit ÷ total staked, with equal stakes and pushes left out.
  • Beat the close is the average CLV: the price taken × the market's closing chance of the pick, with its margin removed, − 1. Positive means the picks were taken at better prices than the market closed at.
  • Over a few dozen picks ROI swings widely: on 30 picks its 95% range is about ±30 points either side. The average CLV settles much sooner, so it is the earlier sign of a real edge.
  • Verdict. With 30 or more picks that have a closing price, a test beats the close or is behind the close when the 95% range of its average CLV sits entirely above or below zero, and is level with the close otherwise. Below 30 picks it is too early to judge.

What is left out

  • When a league or market is taken out of a test, its picks leave that test's record. Each project page shows the leagues and rules its test uses.
  • Testing leagues are tracked apart from the main records.
